About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

A community nonprofit focused on giving animals another chance and connecting them with people who care.

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit focused on giving animals another chance and connecting them with people who care. Our work depends on adopters, foster families, volunteers, donors, and community partners working together to support pets in need.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We treat animals and people with care and respect.

Trust

We share clear information and try to make responsible decisions for every pet.

Community

We believe rescue works best when people work together.

Action

We give people practical ways to adopt, foster, volunteer, donate, and help.

Community Partners

We appreciate the local veterinary teams, pet supply supporters, foster families, volunteers, donors, and community organizations that help make our rescue work possible.
